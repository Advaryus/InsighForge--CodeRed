# AI Data Insights Competitor Product Analyzer

## Overview

The **AI Data Insights Competitor Product Analyzer** is a powerful tool designed to automate competitor analysis, enabling businesses to make data-backed strategic decisions. This project leverages Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Machine Learning (ML) to provide insights into competitor activities, market trends, and customer sentiment.

## Features

- **SWOT Analysis Automation**: Automatically identifies Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats (SWOT) for competitors.
- **Sentiment Analysis**: Utilizes NLP to map social media sentiment trends, highlighting areas of customer praise or concern.
- **Centralized Competitor Data**: Tracks pricing, product updates, and engagement metrics for comprehensive market insights.
- **Predictive Analytics**: Employs ML models to forecast pricing trends and demand patterns, enabling proactive market strategies.
- **Customizable Dashboards**: Creates user-friendly dashboards and downloadable insights for streamlined data visualization and collaboration.
